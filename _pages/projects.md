## Genetics of dermatophytosis

Dermatophyte infections affect the skin, hair, and nails. I investigate their genetic architecture through multi-ancestry GWAS meta-analysis, integrating skin expression QTLs and plasma protein QTLs to prioritize genes and pathways involved in susceptibility. This work connects skin barrier biology and immune regulation with infection risk.

**Approaches:** multi-ancestry GWAS · Mendelian randomization · colocalization · cross-trait analysis

## Proteogenomics of sepsis

I combine large-scale human genetics with plasma proteomics to investigate sepsis susceptibility and host response. A central focus is SERPINA1, which encodes alpha-1 antitrypsin, and the potential role of protease regulation in infection. Genetic and molecular evidence help prioritize candidates for further therapeutic investigation.

**Approaches:** GWAS meta-analysis · cis-pQTL Mendelian randomization · patient proteomics

## Proteomic heterogeneity of complex disease

Clinical diagnoses can encompass distinct underlying biological processes. I use plasma protein profiles, dimensionality reduction, and genetic information to characterize variation within complex diseases, with a focus on type 2 diabetes and related cardiometabolic outcomes.

**Approaches:** plasma proteomics · dimensionality reduction · longitudinal analysis

## Mobile element variation

I am interested in how mobile element variants contribute to human genetic diversity and complex traits, complementing studies focused on single-nucleotide variation.
